## 摘要
经典聚类方法通常假设所有信息特征支持观测数据的单一潜在划分。这一假设对于现代高维数据可能过于严格，因为不同的特征子集可能编码不同的相似性概念，并诱导异质性的样本划分，而某些特征可能不包含有意义的聚类信息。我们开发了一个面向局部聚类的频率学派框架，该框架同时识别特征组并估计与每个特征组关联的样本聚类结构。我们的方法通过标签不变的聚类矩阵表示每个样本划分，并根据特征共享的聚类结构对其进行分组，从而将局部聚类重构为一个特征分组（即聚类之聚类）问题。在异质性次高斯混合模型下，我们构建了特征特定的高斯核相似矩阵，并基于聚类矩阵优化准则提出了一种局部谱聚类程序。所提方法避免了显式似然设定和贝叶斯后验计算，适应异质性特征分布，并允许非信息特征的存在。大量模拟和应用进一步证明了所提方法的实用性和优越性。

## Abstract
Classical clustering methods typically assume that all informative features support a single latent partition of the observations. This assumption can be overly restrictive for modern high-dimensional data, where different subsets of features may encode distinct notions of similarity and induce heterogeneous sample partitions, while some features may contain no meaningful clustering information. We develop a frequentist framework for local clustering that simultaneously identifies feature groups and estimates the sample clustering structure associated with each group. Our approach represents each sample partition by a label-invariant clustering matrix and groups features according to their shared clustering structures, thereby reformulating local clustering as a feature-grouping, or clustering-of-clusterings, problem. Under a heterogeneous sub-Gaussian mixture model, we construct feature-specific Gaussian-kernel similarity matrices and propose a local spectral clustering procedure based on a clustering-matrix optimization criterion. The proposed method avoids explicit likelihood specification and Bayesian posterior computation, accommodates heterogeneous feature distributions, and permits the presence of non-informative features. Extensive simulations and applications further demonstrate the practical utility and superiority of the proposed approach.
